# Request material from inventory

**Who this is for:** Scientists and biologists who need physical compound material for an experiment, and sample management team members who fulfil those requests.

**What you'll accomplish:** You will submit a [sample request](/reference/glossary#sample-request) for a specific compound, track it through approval and preparation, and understand how the sample management team fulfils it.

---

## Before you start

- You need the **Submit sample request** permission to create a request. All workspace members typically have this by default.
- The compound you need must already be [registered](/guides/registration) and have at least one available [sample](/reference/glossary#sample) in inventory. If no material exists, submit a [synthesis request](./synthesis-requests) instead.
- Know approximately how much material you need and have a clear scientific purpose — the sample management team will review this before preparing your material.

<Callout type="info">
A sample request is a formal workflow, not a direct reservation. You are asking the sample management team to locate and prepare material for you. They may choose a different batch than you specified if the preferred batch is unavailable or nearly depleted.
</Callout>

---

## Step 1 — Submit a sample request

1. In the top navigation bar, click **Inventory**.
2. In the left sidebar, click **Sample requests**.
3. Click **New request**.
4. In the **New sample request** form:
   - **Molecule** — search by registration number, name, or structure. Type at least three characters to get suggestions. Select the compound you need.
   - **Batch (optional)** — if you need material from a specific batch (for example, a particular salt form or purity grade), select it from the dropdown. Leave blank to let the sample management team choose the best available batch.
   - **Requested amount** — enter the quantity you need and select the unit (mg, mL, µmol).
   - **Purpose** — describe the scientific purpose concisely. For example: "Activity confirmation in ALDH1A1 TR-FRET assay at 10 µM". This helps the sample management team prioritise and ensures an audit trail.
   - **Priority** — choose `routine`, `urgent`, or `critical`. Use `urgent` and `critical` sparingly; they alert the team immediately.
5. Click **Submit request**.

> 📷 *Screenshot: the New sample request form with the Molecule field showing a search dropdown, the Requested amount field with a unit selector, and the Priority dropdown set to "routine".*

<Callout type="warning">
Submitted sample requests are recorded in the append-only audit trail. The requester, molecule, requested amount, purpose, and timestamp are all captured at submission.
</Callout>

---

## Step 2 — Track your request

After submission, your request appears in **Inventory > Sample requests** with status `submitted`. You can follow its progress through the workflow:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| `submitted` | Awaiting review by the sample management team |
| `approved` | The request has been approved; material will be prepared |
| `preparing` | The team is currently weighing, aliquoting, or diluting your material |
| `fulfilled` | Material is ready; see [Step 4](#step-4--receive-your-material) |
| `rejected` | The request was declined; a rejection reason is shown |
| `cancelled` | The request was withdrawn before preparation began |

> 📷 *Screenshot: the Sample requests list with status badges shown in colour — "submitted" in grey, "approved" in blue, "preparing" in amber, "fulfilled" in green, "rejected" in red.*

<Callout type="default" emoji="💡">
You will receive a notification when your request status changes to `fulfilled` or `rejected`. Check the notification centre (bell icon in the top-right corner) for updates.
</Callout>

## Step 3 — What the sample management team does (fulfiller view)

This section describes the fulfilment workflow for sample management team members.

### Reviewing requests

1. Go to **Inventory > Sample requests**.
2. Filter by status = `submitted` to see all open requests.
3. Click on a request to open its detail view. Review the molecule, requested amount, purpose, and priority.
4. If the request is reasonable and material is available, click **Approve**. If not, click **Reject** and enter a rejection reason — a reason is required so the requester understands what to do next.

<Callout type="info">
Approving or rejecting a request is recorded in the audit trail. The reviewing user and timestamp are captured.
</Callout>

### Locating suitable material

After approval:

1. Open the approved request and click **Start preparing**.
2. The request status changes to `preparing`.
3. In the **Fulfil with sample** section, search for available samples of the requested molecule:
   - chemcellar shows all available samples for that molecule, sorted by amount descending.
   - If the requester specified a batch, those samples appear at the top.
   - Check the storage location to physically locate the sample in the lab.

> 📷 *Screenshot: the fulfilment panel on a sample request detail page, showing a list of available samples with their barcodes, batch numbers, amounts, and storage locations.*

### Aliquoting and recording fulfilment

1. Physically locate the sample in the lab using the storage location shown.
2. Aliquot (or otherwise prepare) the requested amount.
3. Back in chemcellar, open the sample record for the source container:
   - Click **Record consumption** and enter the amount you removed.
   - chemcellar subtracts this from the sample's remaining amount.
4. Return to the sample request and click **Mark as fulfilled**.
5. In the fulfilment dialog:
   - Select the **Sample used** — the barcode of the container you aliquoted from.
   - Confirm the amount actually provided (which may differ slightly from the requested amount if exact weighing was not possible).
6. Click **Confirm fulfilment**.

The request status changes to `fulfilled` and a link to the source sample is recorded on the request. The requester receives a notification.

<Callout type="warning">
Once a request is marked as `fulfilled`, it is a terminal state and cannot be reversed. The sample consumption event and the fulfilment linkage are both recorded in the append-only audit trail. Double-check the sample barcode and amount before confirming.
</Callout>

---

## Step 4 — Receive your material

When you receive a notification that your request is fulfilled:

1. Open the request in **Inventory > Sample requests** and confirm the status is `fulfilled`.
2. The detail page shows:
   - The **sample** that was used to fulfil the request (batch number, barcode, storage location at the time of fulfilment).
   - The **amount actually provided**.
3. Retrieve your material from the agreed collection point and verify the container label matches the barcode shown.

---

## Cancelling a request

You can cancel a request at any time before it reaches `fulfilled`, `rejected`, or `cancelled`:

1. Open the request in **Inventory > Sample requests**.
2. Click **Cancel request**.
3. Confirm the cancellation.

<Callout type="info">
Cancellation is recorded in the audit trail. If preparation was already underway (`preparing` status), inform the sample management team directly so they can stop work.
</Callout>

## Common issues

| Issue | What to do |
|-------|-----------|
| No available samples shown for my molecule | The molecule may have no `available` samples. Check the molecule's **Samples** tab. If all samples are depleted or disposed, submit a [synthesis request](./synthesis-requests) instead. |
| Request rejected with "insufficient material" | The requested amount exceeds what is available. Split your request into smaller amounts or wait for re-synthesis. |
| Request rejected with "batch unavailable" | The specific batch you requested is no longer available. Resubmit without a batch preference to let the team choose. |
| I need material urgently but my request is `submitted` | Change the priority to `urgent` by editing the request (before approval), or contact the sample management team directly. |
